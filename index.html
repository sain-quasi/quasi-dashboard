Lead time & reorder settings — per SKU
Packaging + production + shipping = total lead time. Reorder point = lead time + safety stock buffer.
Seasonality multiplier
Global monthly multiplier applied to forecasted demand during the lead-time window. Defaults to 1.0x (no adjustment) — update once the team confirms patterns.
Connected data sources
Bring in the team's Daily Sales history, China/US/Amazon stock, and real safety-stock days — live if possible, uploaded if not.

One-time setup in Google Sheets: Extensions → Apps Script, paste the provided script, then Deploy → New deployment → Web app (Execute as: Me, Who has access: Anyone). Copy the URL ending in /exec and paste it below.
Or upload the file directly
No setup required — works fully offline. Good as a one-off, or as a fallback if the live connection ever has trouble.
Amazon SKU mapping
Amazon SKU codes detected in your uploaded files, mapped to Quasi's 5 master SKUs. Confirm these are correct — SKU naming differs between platforms.
Planned supply freezes
Known windows where China production stops entirely (e.g. Chinese New Year) — the Forecasting tab won't suggest placing an order during one, and pulls it earlier instead.
Data handling
This dashboard saves your last upload in this browser so it can be reopened without re-uploading. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
